Build a complete, modern, premium corporate website for IBASE TECHNOLOGIES LTD, a Nigerian ICT, telecommunications, networking and technology infrastructure company.

IMPORTANT:
Use the attached IBASE TECHNOLOGIES LTD company profile as the primary source of truth for the company's history, services, capabilities, projects and business information. Do not invent services, certifications, clients, statistics, awards or partnerships that are not supported by the company profile.

The goal is to transform the information in the company profile into a highly professional, credible and modern B2B technology website.
